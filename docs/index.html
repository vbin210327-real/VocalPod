VocalPod 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

VocalPod 帮助你从所选视频中提取声音并在设备上播放。本政策说明 VocalPod 如何处理信息。

视频和音频

你可以通过系统选择器从照片图库或文件中选择视频。VocalPod 仅处理你选择的内容，在设备上提取和分析音频，并将提取的音频及声音库信息保存在设备上。VocalPod 不会将你的视频或音频上传给开发者，也不会修改照片图库中的原视频。

设备上的数据与删除

声音库信息包括音频名称、排序、播放位置和播放设置等，用于提供播放和管理功能。你可以在 App 内删除声音；删除 App 通常也会移除保存在 App 中的数据。已经导出到其他位置的文件需要在相应位置另行删除，设备备份中的副本由备份服务的设置管理。

数据收集与跟踪

VocalPod 不要求注册账号，不向开发者收集个人数据，也不使用广告或跨 App 跟踪。系统、你选择的文件提供方或分享接收方可能按照其各自的隐私政策处理相关信息。

VocalPod Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

VocalPod extracts audio from videos you select and plays it on your device. This policy explains how VocalPod handles information.

Video and audio

You can select videos from your photo library or files using the system picker. VocalPod processes only the content you select, extracts and analyzes audio on your device, and stores the extracted audio and library information on your device. VocalPod does not upload your videos or audio to the developer or modify the original video in your photo library.

On-device data and deletion

Library information includes audio names, ordering, playback positions, and playback settings used for playback and management. You can delete sounds in the app. Deleting the app generally removes data stored in the app. Files already exported elsewhere must be deleted from those locations separately; copies in device backups are managed by your backup service settings.

Data collection and tracking

VocalPod does not require an account, collect personal data for the developer, use advertising, or track you across apps. The operating system, a file provider you choose, or a recipient you share with may handle information under its own privacy policy.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app's features or information practices change. The updated policy and effective date will be published on this page.
